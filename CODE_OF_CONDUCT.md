# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Rebinder contributors and maintainers are expected to create a respectful,
technically rigorous, and harassment-free environment. Be constructive, assume
good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and protect private or sensitive
session data encountered during collaboration.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, and using
project channels to pressure or intimidate others.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release
coordination, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be
reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent practical. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.

Good-faith security research and respectful disagreement about technical or
governance choices are protected forms of participation.
